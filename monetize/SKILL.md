# Monetize

This skill turns a user's career history into a structured monetization plan instead of a one-shot pile of generic advice. It runs a six-module funnel with a direction checkpoint in the middle, verifies market and salary figures with web search where possible, and converges on a single "start this week" action backed by a verifiable 30-day plan. It works in the user's language and adapts currency, platforms, and salary sources to the user's market (China / North America / Europe).

## When to Use This Skill

- The user asks how to monetize their skills or experience, or wants side income / a side hustle
- The user pastes a resume or career history and asks what they could do to earn more
- The user asks about their salary ceiling, higher-paying roles, or career leverage
- The user asks which AI-assisted services they could realistically offer in under 5 hours per week

## What This Skill Does

1. **Background intake**: Parses an uploaded resume or runs a short guided interview (3-4 questions per round: history, resources, goals and constraints), then saves the profile for reuse
2. **Skills audit**: Identifies 10 undervalued skills across four types - hard skills, soft skills, cross-domain combinations, and industry know-how - with who pays and how AI amplifies each
3. **Opportunity scan**: Lists up to 10 income paths with startup cost, time to first customer, weekly hours, difficulty, and a monthly income range, sorted by potential over starting friction
4. **Experience productization**: Converts experience into concrete offers across four formats - services, consulting, digital products, and freelance gigs - each with pricing logic and the role AI plays in delivery
5. **Leverage point**: Converges the whole analysis into ONE action to start this week, plus two alternatives with reasons they were not ranked first
6. **Salary benchmarking and AI starter pack**: Maps higher-paying roles with verified salary ranges and qualification gaps, and packages 5 AI-assisted services startable under 5 hours per week and a small budget

## How to Use

### Basic Usage

```
I've been an accountant for 5 years and want a side income. What are my options?
```

### Advanced Usage

```
Here is my resume: [paste or attach]. Target an extra 5,000 RMB per month,
I have 8 hours a week and a 2,000 RMB budget, and I do not want to appear
on camera. Run the full analysis.
```

```
/monetize 我是做运营的，帮我分析一下薪资天花板
```

Single modules can be requested on their own (salary benchmarking only, side-income scan only); the skill runs just that module.

## Example

**User**: "I've done 6 years of Amazon home-category operations and want an extra 5,000 RMB a month working 8 hours a week."

**Output** (excerpt from a real run):

```

Post a teardown in the seller communities you already frequent: "How I took
a home-category listing from 8% to 14% conversion", offering 3 free
ad-report diagnoses.

Why this one: zero startup cost; reuses your strongest existing channel;
and diagnosis is the standard loss-leader in this market, so one post feeds

## 7. 30-day action plan (week 1)

| When  | Action                                  | Acceptance criteria              |
| ----- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Wed   | Write the teardown post                 | Post live, 3 before/after data   |
| Thu   | Publish in community                    | 5+ interested replies            |

```

## Tips

- Every market or salary figure is either web-verified with a source or explicitly flagged as an estimate; estimates are collected in an appendix
- Let the skill run modules 1-2 first and check the direction before requesting the rest
- Income figures are ranges, and the first three months typically land at the bottom of the range or at zero
- The skill declines gray-area plays (fake reviews, content laundering, unlicensed financial advice) and says why

## Common Use Cases

- A mid-career professional exploring a first side income around a full-time job
- A resume-holder deciding between freelancing, consulting, or digital products
- An employee benchmarking whether switching roles beats building a side business
